Connect your ToGrill AT-02 BBQ thermometer straight to Homey Pro over Bluetooth LE — no hub, no cloud, and no companion app. Live temperatures for every meat probe and the grill's ambient sensor appear right on your Homey dashboard.

Set a target temperature per probe and let Homey Flows alert you the moment it's reached, warn you if a probe disconnects, or sound the alarm if the grill runs too hot. Battery level is monitored so you're never caught out mid-cook, and everything runs locally over Bluetooth.
